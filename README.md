COFFEE-SHOP-SALES-ANALYSIS

Project Overview
Analysis of coffee shop sales data to evaluate overall business performance. Identifying the best selling products and most profitable
product categories. Understand sale trends by day,hour and month.
Determine peak sales periods and customer purchasing patterns.
Provision of data-driven recommendations to improve sales and business perfomance.

Methodology
Data collection, imported the coffee shop sales for analysis.
Data cleaning ,checked missing values and duplicate records.
Data preparation,organised the data.
Exploratory Data Analysis, examined sales revenue, product quantities,transaction patterns.
KPI Analysis, Evaluated key-perfomance indicators.
Data Visualisation
Business recommendation

Summary
Revenue rises steadily from February to June.
Coffee and tea are the strongest revenue engines they drive category mix.
Morning is the busiest service window.
All three store contribute almost equally eg Hells Kitchen leads at 33.84%  followed by Astoria at 33.23%
and Lower Manhattan at 32.92%.

Recommendations
Continue promoting the products that performed well from March to June.
Focus on popular products and ensure sufficient stock is available.
Monitor monthly revenue to make progress. 
Improve sales in under performing months and stores.
